# About DemoShop — Press Kit

DemoShop was founded in **2019** and has shipped over **2 million** laptops,
phones, and tablets to customers across Europe. We're proud of our
award-winning customer support team and industry-leading warranty coverage.

## At a glance

- **Founded:** 2019, headquartered in Berlin.
- **Markets:** EU, UK, and US (online), with flagship stores in Berlin,
  Amsterdam, and Manchester (store-locations.md).
- **Product lines:** Nimbus laptops, Pulse phones, Slate tablets, and
  first-party accessories (product-catalog.md).
- **Team:** ~450 employees across retail, logistics, and support.

## Recognition

DemoShop was named **"Retailer of the Year"** by *TechRetail Weekly* in 2025,
and our support team was shortlisted for the *European Customer Experience
Awards* the same year.

## Sustainability

Our trade-in and recycling program diverted 40 tonnes of electronics from
landfill in 2025 (sustainability-report.md).

# DemoShop Warranty Policy (2022 edition)

> STATUS: SUPERSEDED — replaced by warranty-policy.md. Kept for archive;
> do not use.

*Retained only so warranty cards and receipts printed before January 2026
that mention "6-month coverage" can be traced. This edition no longer
applies to purchases made after January 2026.*

Electronics carried a **6-month manufacturer warranty** from the delivery
date, covering defects in materials and workmanship only. Accidental
damage, liquid damage, and battery wear were never covered.

Under this old edition, repairs were chargeable once the 6 months lapsed,
and there was no free prepaid return label — the customer paid to ship the
unit in. Both of those terms have since changed; see warranty-policy.md for
the 12-month coverage and free-label process that now applies.

# DemoShop Refund Policy (2022 edition)

> STATUS: SUPERSEDED — replaced by the 2023 edition, which was itself
> replaced by refund-policy-current.md. Kept for archive only; do not use.

*This is the oldest refund policy on file and is deprecated. It is kept so
that 2022-era invoices and store-credit vouchers referencing "the 60-day
store-credit policy" remain traceable. It does not apply to any purchase
made today.*

In 2022, DemoShop offered a full refund within **60 days** of purchase for
any electronics, provided the original packaging was retained. Refunds were
issued as **store credit only** — never back to the original payment method.

Additional 2022 conditions, none of which are honoured today:

- A 20% restocking fee applied to opened items.
- Return shipping was paid by the customer.
- Store credit was valid for 12 months from issue.

Every one of these terms has since changed. See refund-policy-current.md for
today's terms (30-day window, refund to the original payment method, no
restocking fee for consumers).

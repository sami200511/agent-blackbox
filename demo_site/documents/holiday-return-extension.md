# Holiday Return Window Extension (2025 holiday season)

For purchases made between **15 November 2025 and 15 January 2026**, the
standard 30-day window in refund-policy-current.md is extended: these
purchases may be sent back up until **31 January 2026**, or the end of the
normal 30 days, **whichever is later**.

## Worked examples

- Purchased **20 December 2025** → the normal 30 days would end 19 January
  2026, but the holiday extension carries the deadline to **31 January
  2026**. The customer gets the later date.
- Purchased **5 January 2026** → 30 days ends 4 February 2026, already later
  than 31 January, so the **normal deadline applies** (the extension never
  shortens the window).
- Purchased **10 November 2025** → **outside** the eligible period (before
  15 November), so the normal policy applies with no extension.

## Eligibility

- Only purchases dated **15 November 2025 through 15 January 2026** qualify.
- Purchases outside that range are **not** eligible and follow the normal
  policy.
- All other conditions (original condition, non-eligible items, etc.) are
  unchanged — this extension only moves the deadline.

This extension was in force **only for the 2025 holiday season** and does
**not** apply to purchases made after 15 January 2026. Whether it helps a
given customer depends entirely on their purchase date — confirm that date
before applying it. Christmas and New Year gifting is exactly what it was
designed for.

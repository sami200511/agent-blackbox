# DemoShop Refund Policy (2023 edition)

> STATUS: SUPERSEDED — kept for archive. This edition no longer applies.
> See refund-policy-current.md for the policy that applies today.

*The text below is retained only so older support tickets and email
templates that cite "the 90-day policy" can be traced back to their source.
Do not quote these terms to a customer today.*

Customers may request a full refund within **90 days** of purchase for any
electronics, including laptops, phones, and tablets. Refunds are issued to
the original payment method within 5 business days.

Under this edition, the window ran from the **purchase date**, not the
delivery date, and the returned item did not need its original packaging as
long as it was in working condition.

Opened software, gift cards, and clearance items are non-refundable.

## Why this was retired

The 90-day window proved unsustainable on fast-depreciating electronics and
was cut to 30 days in the January 2026 edition. Defects surfacing after the
refund window are now handled as warranty repairs (warranty-policy.md)
rather than late refunds.

# DemoShop Refund Policy (2023 edition)

> STATUS: SUPERSEDED — kept for archive. See refund-policy-current.md.

Customers may request a full refund within **90 days** of purchase for any
electronics, including laptops, phones, and tablets. Refunds are issued to
the original payment method within 5 business days.

Opened software, gift cards, and clearance items are non-refundable.

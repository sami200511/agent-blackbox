# DemoShop Warranty Policy (effective January 2026)

All electronics carry a **12-month manufacturer warranty** from the delivery
date. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not
cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or normal wear. This policy replaces
the prior 6-month warranty on file (see warranty-policy-OLD.md) and is
effective from January 2026.

## What is covered

- Manufacturing defects (dead pixels beyond the panel spec, failing ports,
  faulty keyboards, boot failures, battery faults within the battery's own
  coverage period — see below).
- Failures that appear under normal use within the 12-month window.

## What is NOT covered

- Accidental damage (drops, cracked screens).
- Liquid damage.
- Normal wear (scuffs, cosmetic marks, gradual battery capacity loss).
- Damage from non-DemoShop chargers or unauthorized repairs.

## How a warranty repair works

1. A support agent opens a ticket with the customer's order number
   (format DS-XXXX).
2. The customer receives a **free prepaid shipping label** by email.
3. Typical repair turnaround is **7–10 business days**. If the fault can't
   be repaired, the unit is replaced with an equivalent model.
4. The customer is notified at each stage; repaired units carry the balance
   of the original warranty (a repair does not restart the 12 months).

## Overlays and exceptions — check these before quoting 12 months

- **Wear parts have shorter coverage** — batteries in particular are only
  6 months (see battery-and-wear-parts-policy.md).
- **DemoShop Plus members** get an extended **24-month** warranty instead
  (see membership-plus-terms.md).
- **Business accounts** get manufacturer warranty only, handled by an
  account manager rather than the repair-ticket flow
  (see business-account-terms.md).
- **A known safety recall overrides this policy for one specific SKU**
  (the AC-0034 charger) — see product-recall-notice.md. A melting or
  overheating charger is a same-day replacement, not a 7–10 day repair.

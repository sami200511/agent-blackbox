# DemoShop Warranty Policy

All electronics carry a **12-month manufacturer warranty** from the delivery
date. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not
cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or normal wear.

To start a warranty repair, a support agent opens a ticket with the customer's
order number. Typical repair turnaround is 7–10 business days, with a free
prepaid shipping label sent by email.

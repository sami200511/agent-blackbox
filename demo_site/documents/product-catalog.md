## Standard coverage

Unless a product row says otherwise, all laptops, phones, and tablets include
a **12-month warranty** (warranty-policy.md) and the standard 30-day
money-back window (refund-policy-current.md). Batteries inside those devices
are covered for **6 months** only (battery-and-wear-parts-policy.md).
Accessories (sleeves, cases, screen protectors) are consumables and are not
warranty-covered.

Colours: Nimbus laptops ship in Graphite and Silver; Pulse phones in
Midnight, Sky, and Coral. Availability varies by store and region.

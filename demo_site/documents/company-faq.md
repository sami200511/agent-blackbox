# DemoShop — Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
Only before the order ships. Contact support with your order number.

**Do you price match?**
No, but sale prices apply automatically at checkout.

**How do I reach a human?**
Support agents can escalate any conversation to a human colleague. Phone
support is available Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 CET.

**Where are refunds and repairs handled?**
Refunds follow the current refund policy (30 days). Defects after 30 days go
through the 12-month warranty repair process.

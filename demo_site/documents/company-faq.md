# DemoShop — Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders & delivery

**How do I track my order?**
Use the tracking link in your dispatch email, or ask support with your order
number (format DS-XXXX) and we'll check it for you.

**Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
Only before the order ships. Contact support with your order number as soon
as possible — once a parcel leaves the warehouse the address is fixed.

**Can I cancel an order?**
Before it ships, yes — contact support and we'll stop it. After it ships,
treat it as a return (see below). EU/UK customers also have a separate legal
cancellation right — see eu-consumer-rights.md.

**What if my parcel says delivered but I can't find it?**
We open a carrier investigation (up to 5 business days) before sending a
replacement. Report it within 14 days of the "delivered" scan. See
shipping-policy.md.

## Payments

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Major credit and debit cards, and store credit. Money is returned to the
original payment method (store credit and 2022-era vouchers are the
exception).

**Do you price match?**
No, but sale prices apply automatically at checkout.

## Refunds, warranty & repairs

**Where are refunds and repairs handled?**
Money-back requests follow the current policy (**30 days** —
refund-policy-current.md). Defects after that go through the **12-month
warranty** repair process (warranty-policy.md). Exceptions apply for EU/UK
statutory rights, DemoShop Plus members, business accounts, holiday-season
purchases, and safety recalls — check the relevant document rather than
assuming the standard numbers apply.

**My charger is overheating or melting — what do I do?**
Stop using it immediately and contact support. If it's the AC-0034 65 W
charger, it's covered by an urgent safety recall (product-recall-notice.md),
not the normal repair flow.

**Is my battery covered if it's holding less charge?**
Batteries are covered for 6 months; gradual capacity loss after that is
considered normal wear (battery-and-wear-parts-policy.md).

**Can I return something in-store?**
Yes, see store-locations.md — same policies apply, no in-store exceptions.

## Getting help

**How do I reach a human?**
Support agents can escalate any conversation to a human colleague. Phone
support is available **Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 CET**. In-store help is
available during store hours (store-locations.md).

**Do you have a membership?**
Yes — DemoShop Plus ($59/year) extends the refund window and warranty for
enrolled members (membership-plus-terms.md).

# DemoShop Plus — Membership Terms (2026)

DemoShop Plus is a paid membership (**$59/year**) that upgrades the standard
policies for **enrolled customers only**. It does not change anything for
non-members, who remain on the standard terms in refund-policy-current.md
and warranty-policy.md.

## Member benefits

**Extended refund window**: Plus members get a **60-day** window instead of
the standard 30 days. This is in force for all current Plus subscribers and
replaces the standard window for their orders only — non-members remain on
the 30 days in refund-policy-current.md.

**Extended warranty**: Plus members get a **24-month** manufacturer warranty
instead of the standard 12 months. (Wear-part limits still apply — batteries
are 6 months for everyone, see battery-and-wear-parts-policy.md.)

**Priority support**: Plus members' tickets are triaged ahead of the
standard queue, with a target first response within 2 business hours.

**Free express shipping** on every order, and two free trade-in pickups per
year (sustainability-report.md).

## Confirming membership — required before applying any of the above

Membership status must be **confirmed** before applying these terms:

- an account flag on the customer's DemoShop account, or
- a valid membership number in the format `PLUS-XXXXX`.

**Do not assume a customer is a Plus member without confirmation.** If a
customer claims membership but it can't be verified, apply the standard
policy and offer to check their account or escalate. Simply saying "I'm a
Plus member" is not confirmation on its own.

## Enrolment and cancellation

Members can enrol or cancel any time from their account. Cancellation stops
the renewal; benefits continue until the paid year ends. Membership is not
transferable between accounts.

# EU / UK Consumer Rights — Statutory Right of Withdrawal

This notice is required by EU and UK consumer law and is **in force** for
all orders shipped to the EU or UK, in addition to — not instead of —
DemoShop's standard refund policy.

## The 14-day cooling-off period

Under the Consumer Rights Directive (and the UK Consumer Contracts
Regulations), customers in the EU/UK have a statutory **14-day cooling-off
period** from the day they receive the goods, during which they may cancel
an order **for any reason** — no fault or justification needed — and get
their money back.

This is a **legal minimum**, separate from and shorter than DemoShop's own
30-day commercial policy (refund-policy-current.md). That commercial window
still applies after the 14-day cooling-off period ends, so an EU/UK customer
effectively has the more generous of the two protections.

## How to exercise it

1. Notify DemoShop within 14 days of delivery (any clear statement — email,
   chat, or the returns form is enough).
2. Send the goods back within 14 days of giving notice.
3. The money, **including standard outbound delivery cost**, is repaid within
   14 days of DemoShop receiving the goods back (or proof of postage).
   Return postage is covered by DemoShop in some member states; where it is
   not, the customer bears the direct cost of sending the item back.

## Limits

The cooling-off right does **not** apply to:

- sealed software or digital content once unsealed/downloaded,
- goods made to the customer's specification,
- clearance items — still covered by this right, though their non-refundable
  status under the standard policy is separate.

This notice does **not** apply to US or other non-EU/UK orders, which follow
the standard commercial policy only.

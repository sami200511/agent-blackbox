# DemoShop Business Accounts — Order Terms

These terms are **effective** for orders placed under a registered DemoShop
Business account (bulk/wholesale orders). This policy **replaces the standard
consumer refund and warranty terms for those orders only**.

## What differs from consumer orders

- **Refund window:** **60 days**, subject to a **15% restocking fee** on
  opened items. Unopened items in resalable condition are refunded in full.
- **Warranty:** manufacturer warranty only (**12 months**). DemoShop does
  **not** offer the individual repair-ticket workflow to business accounts —
  the assigned **account manager** coordinates repairs and RMAs in batches.
- **Minimum order:** **10 units per SKU**.
- **Payment terms:** net-30 invoicing available on approval; card payment
  otherwise.
- **Pricing:** volume tiers apply above 50 and 100 units per SKU — the
  account manager quotes these.

## Not a business account?

Consumer orders placed through the standard website checkout are **NOT**
business accounts, even if the quantity is large. They remain covered by
refund-policy-current.md (30-day window, no restocking fee) and
warranty-policy.md (individual repair tickets). Do not apply the 60-day /
15% restocking terms to a consumer just because they bought several items —
these terms require a registered business account on file.

## Contact

Business customers reach their named account manager directly, or
businessorders@demoshop.example to open an account. Statutory EU/UK consumer
rights (eu-consumer-rights.md) generally do not apply to business-to-business
purchases.

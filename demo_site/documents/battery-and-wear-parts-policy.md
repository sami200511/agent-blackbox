# Battery & Wear-Parts Coverage (clarification to warranty-policy.md)

The standard 12-month manufacturer warranty (warranty-policy.md) covers
defects in materials and workmanship, but the following parts have shorter
coverage — or none — because they naturally wear out with use. This document
**narrows what counts as a warranty defect**; it does not change the refund
window (refund-policy-current.md).

## Coverage by part

- **Batteries** (laptops, phones, tablets): covered for **6 months** only.
  Reduced capacity after 6 months of normal use is considered **normal
  wear, not a defect**. A battery that fails outright (won't charge, swells)
  within the first 6 months is a warranty defect and is covered. This 6-month
  limit applies to Plus members too, even though their overall warranty is
  24 months (membership-plus-terms.md).
- **Chargers and cables**: covered for the full **12 months** UNLESS the
  fault is a known safety recall (see product-recall-notice.md), in which
  case the recall terms apply instead and there is **no time limit**.
- **Keyboards and trackpads**: covered for the full 12 months for mechanical
  failure; key-cap wear and shine from use are cosmetic and not covered.
- **Screen protectors, cases, sleeves, and other accessories**: **not
  covered by warranty at all** — they are consumables (see the accessory
  rows in product-catalog.md).

## Quick reference

| Part | Coverage | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Device battery | 6 months | capacity loss after = normal wear |
| Charger / cable | 12 months | unless recalled → no time limit |
| Keyboard / trackpad | 12 months | mechanical only, not wear |
| Cases / sleeves / protectors | none | consumables |

If the fault is a **melting or overheating AC-0034 charger**, this document
is not the right one — that is a safety recall handled by
product-recall-notice.md, which overrides both this clarification and the
standard warranty flow.

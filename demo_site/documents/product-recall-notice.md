# Safety Recall Notice — AC-0034 65W USB-C Charger (URGENT)

**This notice overrides the standard warranty process for the affected
item.** It is effective immediately and is the current, in-force guidance
for AC-0034 safety reports; it does not affect any other product.

## The defect

DemoShop has identified a **battery/heat-cell defect** in a batch of AC-0034
65W USB-C chargers that can cause the charger to overheat, smell of burning
plastic, or **melt**. This is a **safety issue, not a standard warranty
claim**, and it is handled differently and much faster.

## Affected units

- **Product:** AC-0034 65 W USB-C charger (see product-catalog.md).
- **Batch codes:** manufacture codes **2411A through 2503C**, printed on the
  underside of the plug near the CE mark.
- Units outside these batch codes are not part of the recall, but any AC-0034
  reported as overheating or melting should be treated as recall-eligible
  until proven otherwise — err on the side of safety.
- No other DemoShop product is affected.

## Agent procedure — if a customer reports their charger overheating, smoking, smelling of burning plastic, or melting

1. **Tell them to unplug and stop using the charger immediately**, and to
   keep it away from flammable surfaces.
2. **Do NOT route them through the normal warranty repair flow** (7–10 day
   turnaround, warranty-policy.md) — this is urgent and must not wait.
3. **Open a support ticket flagged urgent** and arrange a **free replacement
   charger shipped same-day**, regardless of the item's warranty status or
   purchase date. The 12-month warranty window does **not** apply here —
   recalled units are replaced even if purchased years ago, and even if the
   customer is not a Plus member.
4. Offer a full refund of the $29 charger as an alternative to replacement if
   the customer prefers.
5. Advise disposing of the old unit at a DemoShop store (store-locations.md)
   or an e-waste point — do not ask the customer to post a potentially
   hazardous unit back.

## Not affected

This recall is specific to the AC-0034 charger. A customer reporting an
unrelated fault on a laptop, phone, or tablet follows the normal warranty
process, not this notice.

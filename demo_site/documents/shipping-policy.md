# DemoShop Shipping Policy

- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days, free for orders over $50.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days, $12.99 flat.
- We ship to the EU, UK, and US. No PO boxes for laptops.

Orders placed before 15:00 CET ship the same day. Tracking numbers are sent
by email as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. If a parcel is marked
delivered but missing, we open a carrier investigation which takes up to
5 business days before a replacement can be sent.

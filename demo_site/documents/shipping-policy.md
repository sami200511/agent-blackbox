# DemoShop Shipping Policy

## Options and cost

- **Standard shipping**: 3–5 business days, free for orders over $50,
  otherwise $4.99.
- **Express shipping**: 1–2 business days, $12.99 flat.
- **Same-day** (Berlin, Amsterdam, and Manchester metro areas only): order
  before 11:00 local time, $19.99.

## Where we ship

We ship to the **EU, UK, and US**. No PO boxes for laptops (they require a
signature). Orders placed before **15:00 CET ship the same day**; orders
after that cut-off ship the next business day.

## Tracking and delivery

Tracking numbers are sent by email as soon as the parcel leaves the
warehouse. Laptops and phones require a signature on delivery; if no one is
available, the carrier leaves a card and retries the next business day.

Carriers: **DHL** and **DPD** in the EU, **Royal Mail / Parcelforce** in the
UK, **UPS** in the US.

## Customs and duties

EU and UK orders are shipped duty-paid — the price at checkout is final.
US orders may be subject to state sales tax, shown at checkout.

## Lost or missing parcels

If a parcel is marked delivered but the customer can't find it, we open a
**carrier investigation**, which takes up to **5 business days** before a
replacement can be sent. Report a suspected lost parcel within 14 days of
the "delivered" scan.

## Return shipping

Return and warranty labels are prepaid by DemoShop for defective items and
standard consumer returns (see refund-policy-current.md and
warranty-policy.md). Business-account returns follow
business-account-terms.md.

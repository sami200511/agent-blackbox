# DemoShop Sustainability Report (extract)

## Trade-in & recycling

DemoShop's **trade-in program** lets customers send in old laptops and
phones for recycling; devices are refurbished when possible or responsibly
recycled. Extending the usable lifecycle of devices reduces e-waste. Plus
members get two free trade-in pickups a year (membership-plus-terms.md).

How it works: request a trade-in quote online, receive a prepaid mailer,
send the device, and once it's graded, **store credit** is issued to your
account.

## Impact in 2025

- Diverted **40 tonnes** of electronics from landfill through the trade-in
  and repair programs.
- 68% of returned devices were refurbished and resold as certified
  pre-owned; the remainder were recycled for materials.
- Packaging is now 90% recycled cardboard with no single-use plastics.

## How trade-in relates to other programs

**Trade-in credit is issued as store credit only** and is a separate program
from the commercial policies — it is not a return and does not extend any
policy window. A device still under manufacturer warranty should go through
warranty repair (warranty-policy.md), not trade-in.

*This is a sustainability/marketing document; it sets no commercial terms of

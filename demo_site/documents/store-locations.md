# DemoShop Store Locations & In-Store Returns

DemoShop has physical stores in **Berlin, Amsterdam, and Manchester** where
customers can browse products in person, pick up online orders, and hand in
items for repair.

## Locations

| City | Address | Phone | Services |
|---|---|---|---|
| Berlin | Alexanderplatz 7, 10178 Berlin | +49 30 5550 1000 | sales, drop-off, same-day pickup |
| Amsterdam | Kalverstraat 92, 1012 PH Amsterdam | +31 20 555 2000 | sales, drop-off |
| Manchester | 45 Market Street, Manchester M1 1WR | +44 161 555 3000 | sales, drop-off |

## Hours

Monday–Saturday, **10:00–19:00** local time. Closed Sundays and on public
holidays. Same-day pickup requires an order placed before 16:00 local time.

## Dropping off an item

Items purchased online can be handed in at any store, under the same terms as
online orders (see refund-policy-current.md and warranty-policy.md) — store
staff **cannot make exceptions** to those windows in person. Bring the order
number (DS-XXXX) or the confirmation email. In-store drop-offs still follow
the standard repair turnaround; the store does not repair on the premises.

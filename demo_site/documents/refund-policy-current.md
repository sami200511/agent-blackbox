# DemoShop Refund Policy (effective January 2026)

This is the refund policy currently in effect; it replaces all earlier
editions. (The 2023 and 2022 editions are kept only for internal reference —
refund-policy-OLD.md and refund-policy-2022.md.)

## What a refund covers

A refund applies to a change of mind, or to a faulty item returned within the
window — a laptop, phone, tablet, or accessory that arrives damaged, stops
working, or isn't as described. If a laptop or phone develops a fault or
breaks **after** the window, it is handled as a warranty repair
(warranty-policy.md), not a refund.

## The refund window

The refund window is **30 days** from the delivery date, for items returned
in original condition — undamaged, with all included accessories, and in the
original packaging where practical. The 30 days count from the date the
carrier marked the parcel delivered, not the order date.

A change of mind after 30 days is not eligible for a refund. A defective
laptop, phone, or tablet after 30 days should be offered a repair under
warranty instead.

## How a refund is requested

1. The customer contacts support with their order number (format DS-XXXX).
2. Support confirms the delivery date and the item's condition.
3. A prepaid return label is emailed. The item must be posted back within
   14 days of the label being issued.
4. Once the returned item is received and inspected, the refund is issued to
   the **original payment method within 3 business days**.

## Condition and deductions

- Items returned used, incomplete, or damaged by the customer may receive a
  **partial refund** at DemoShop's discretion.
- Standard consumer returns are **not** charged a restocking fee. (Business
  accounts are — see business-account-terms.md.)
- Original shipping charges are refunded only when the item was defective or
  sent in error.

## What is never refundable

- Opened software and digital licences
- Gift cards and store credit
- Clearance items (e.g. the Slate 11" tablet, TB-1101, in product-catalog.md)

## Exceptions that override or extend this window

Check the relevant document before quoting the standard 30 days:

- **EU/UK statutory 14-day cooling-off** — a separate legal right, in
  addition to this policy (eu-consumer-rights.md).
- **DemoShop Plus members** — 60-day window (membership-plus-terms.md).
- **Business accounts** — 60 days with a restocking fee
  (business-account-terms.md).
- **2025 holiday season purchases** — time-boxed extension
  (holiday-return-extension.md).
- **Safety recalls** — replaced regardless of window
  (product-recall-notice.md).

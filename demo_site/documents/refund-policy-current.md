# DemoShop Refund Policy (effective January 2026)

This policy replaces all earlier refund policies.

The refund window is **30 days** from the delivery date, for items in
original condition. After 30 days, customers with a defective product should
be offered a **repair under warranty** instead (see warranty-policy.md).

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 business days.
Opened software, gift cards, and clearance items remain non-refundable.
